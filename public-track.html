How to read this track

Annual and accumulated bills

The bill estimate uses the verified charge for your LSOA and band. Each calendar year combines January–March at the preceding fiscal-year charge and April–December at the new charge. Accumulated bills add those yearly estimates in nominal pounds.

This is an estimate of the standard property liability, not a record of what you personally paid or your share of a shared home’s bill.

Average effective tax rate

Each annual rate combines that bill with sale prices for the same band and year in your LSOA. If fewer than five sales are available, the tracker uses the same-year, same-band MSOA sample with at least five sales. If neither is available, that year’s rate stays missing.

If several stages cover one year, their bill estimates and ETR estimates are averaged separately with equal weights. Two stages receive 1/2 each, three receive 1/3 each. The calendar year contributes once to totals and period averages. If a measure is unavailable for any residence, that annual measure stays missing. The period average gives equal weight to each available year. It is not a sum of percentages. England is compared over exactly those years using all bands in the same prepared transaction sample.

Data and comparison details

Prices and bills describe transacting homes. Your home’s actual value can differ from the local sample. The rate is the bill multiplied by the mean reciprocal sale price, expressed in percent; it is not the bill divided by an arithmetic mean price.

LSOA2021 and MSOA2021 boundaries are fixed through time. The tracker uses the prepared model sample with the saved annual upper-ETR exclusions and verified historical local charge routes. A historical route with conflicting charges is left unresolved. No council’s charge is substituted to fill an unresolved local bill.

The England, local-authority and MSOA comparisons use transaction-weighted means within each year. The tracker’s calendar-year charging rule and prepared sample differ from the paper’s transaction-date fiscal-year calculations, so its reference figures can differ slightly from Research.

Annual gaps stay missing. Bill totals and average rates may cover different numbers of years; each comparison states its coverage. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are outside this England dataset.

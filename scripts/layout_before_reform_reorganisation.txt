<section id="methods" class="section methods"><div><p class="eyebrow">05 / READ THE EVIDENCE</p><h2>What these numbers mean</h2><p>This atlas accompanies research on England’s frozen Council Tax base. It presents existing analysis results and aggregate calculations from the saved analysis samples.</p><p class="note">Prepared by Sofie R. Waltl and Shizhen Wang · Research preview, 29 October 2026</p></div><div><details open><summary>Samples and the effective tax rate</summary><p>ETR = 100 × standard annual liability / observed sale price. Only observations strictly above each year’s 99.5th current-ETR percentile are excluded. Ties and the entire lower tail are retained. There is no price trimming, winsorisation or policy-specific filtering. Transactions, including repeated sales, are not a stock census.</p></details><details><summary>Reform rules and revenue neutrality</summary><p>Options 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 preserve baseline revenue separately in each LAD transaction sample; Option 3 adds a surcharge. Prices and behaviour are held fixed. Rebanding targets official September-2026 local stock shares retrospectively, using fixed LAD2026 groups. Local median-price multipliers are not themselves bill changes; revenue rescaling also matters. High-value thresholds use the saved April-2026 uprating assumptions.</p></details><details><summary>Geography, missing values and colour scales</summary><p>All 33,755 English LSOA2021 areas remain in the map, including no-sale areas. LSOA display geometry is simplified at 50 metres; the statistical values are unchanged. All 296 LAD2026 result codes match the retained LAD2025 reference polygons; this verifies code coverage rather than identical boundary vintages. Ambiguous or unobserved LSOA-to-LAD links are not assigned arbitrarily. Current-level colour classes are pooled across years; policy-change scales are fixed across years and policies. Saturated end colours include changes beyond the legend endpoints.</p></details><details><summary>Limits to interpretation</summary><p>Current band snapshots do not provide complete historical assignment records. Standard liabilities exclude household discounts, exemptions, support and premiums. Neighbourhood income has incomplete denominator provenance; deprivation uses fixed Census2021 attributes. No causal tax-price effects, household-income incidence, bootstrap uncertainty or full-stock revenue neutrality are implied.</p></details><details><summary>Sources and aggregate downloads</summary><p>HM Land Registry PPD; collected valuation-list records; official local Band D charges and stock shares; retained ONS geographical references. Boundary data contain public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Detailed source paths, processing and checks are recorded in the accompanying README and validation report.</p><div class="downloads"><a href="downloads/lsoa_current_1995_2025.csv.gz">LSOA current tax, 1995–2025 ↓</a><a href="downloads/lsoa_policy_2022_2025.csv.gz">LSOA reform results ↓</a><a href="downloads/lad_policy_2022_2025.csv.gz">LAD reform results ↓</a><a href="DATA_VALIDATION.json">Validation report ↓</a></div></details></div></section>
